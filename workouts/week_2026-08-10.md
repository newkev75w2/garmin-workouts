## The volume jump — read this

You're averaging **3 km / 0.7 runs per week** over 90 days. This week is roughly
**11 km across 3 runs** — over triple. That is more than a sensible ramp normally allows.

It's workable only because two of the three are 20 minutes and genuinely easy. Guardrails:

- **The easy runs are the droppable ones.** If readiness is under 40 or Body Battery is
  low, skip the evening run and keep the lift. Never the other way round.
- **Hold at this volume for 2–3 weeks** before adding a fourth run or extending any of them.
- **Recovery is already marginal** — readiness averaged ~50 last week (25, 41, 65, 64, 50, 34, 66),
  Body Battery is 12 today. If it trends down two weeks running, cut back to one evening run.
