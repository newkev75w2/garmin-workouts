## Running — one session, Thursday evening

**10 min warm-up → 4 × 3 min @ 190–196 bpm, 3 min jog between → 5 min cool-down.**
~35 min, ~6 km.

Thursday because legs are three days clear of Monday — the freshest they'll be. Nothing
follows it to compromise.

**Why hard rather than easy:** your distribution is bad (10% easy, 50% grey zone against a
~75% easy target), but that target applies to a multi-run week. One easy run in seven days
does little for aerobic base — base needs frequency and volume. One hard run delivers a real
VO2max stimulus, and yours is climbing: **36.2 (May) → 43.5 (10 Aug)**. Kept to 4×3min rather
than 5 because it's your fourth straight lifting day and you fly the next morning.

Zones off max HR 211: easy ≤162 · grey 163–180 (avoid) · threshold 181–193 · VO2max 194+.

**This project cannot generate run workout files.** `workout.py` hardcodes
`sportTypeId: 5` (strength_training). Extending it to support running is a separate job —
say the word.
